This is the first entry. The site is a place to write things down — small notes,
longer essays, project logs, and reading.

## Why a blog

Writing is a way to think. The structure of an audience, even an imaginary one,
forces clarity that solo notebooks rarely demand.

> "Writing is nature's way of letting you know how sloppy your thinking is."
> — Guindon

## What to expect

- Short posts on engineering and tools I use.
- Notes from books and papers.
- Occasional longer pieces.

That's it. Welcome.
