Minimalism in design is not the absence of decisions. It is decisions made so
deliberately that nothing else is needed.

A serif headline. A small caps nav. A single rule across the page.
The page reads like a piece of paper.

## Three rules

1. Type before color.
2. Hierarchy before ornament.
3. Whitespace before everything.

That is the whole essay.
