I am a data scientist by trade and a storyteller at heart. Over the past decade, I've lived ferociously across three continents, from nine years in Singapore, to a pandemic year in Shanghai, before landing in the United States in 2021. Today, I live in New York City, where I find myself constantly evaluating how the landscape of AI is redefining what it means to be a data scientist, or maybe I won't be soon, who knows?

Beyond the data, my eyes are usually fixed on the broader world and the artistic side of human emotions. When I was younger, I once rode a green train to the border of Inner Mongolia with a last-minute ticket and no seat, hauling four heavy boxes of equatorial mounts and counterweights just to chase the Transit of Venus. These days, I favor simpler stargazing, long hikes, tennis, and snowboarding.

My greatest current adventure, however, is a literary one. While I have long written prose and columns in Chinese, I am now navigating the clumsy, thrilling transition of learning to express my creative voice in English.
